Privacy, Security and Agreement

Javanie's Studio, a Javanie Enterprise Limited company. Last updated: add the date when this goes live.

What we collect

When you send an inquiry, we collect your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, and the project details you choose to share, such as location, land size, budget range, timeline and ideas.

Why we collect it

We use your details only to reply to your inquiry, discuss your project, and prepare a quote or proposal. We do not sell your details or share them with advertisers.

Who sees it

Your inquiry is received by Javanie's Studio. It may pass through the email, WhatsApp and messaging services you use to send it, and those services have their own privacy rules.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries only as long as needed to respond and to carry out any work you hire us for. If no project follows, we aim to delete your details within 12 months.

Your rights

  • Ask for a copy of the details we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your details.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.

To make a request, contact us through the email or WhatsApp number on our website.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including secure connections on our website and limited access to inquiries. No method of sending information online is completely secure, so please do not send passwords or payment details through the inquiry form.

Your designs and ours

All designs, drawings, renders and plans shown on this website remain the property of Javanie's Studio and may not be copied or reused without written permission. Concept ideas and any tool outputs on this site are rough guides only. They are not construction documents and are not a substitute for professionally prepared, approved drawings.

Agreement for work

Sending an inquiry does not create a contract. Any work will be agreed separately in writing, covering the scope, fees, timeline and approvals needed.

Cookies

This site does not currently use tracking cookies. If that changes, this page and a cookie notice will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this page can be sent to us through the inquiry form or our WhatsApp number.
